What are Out of Commerce Works (DNNT) and how can I access the document?

Out of Commerce Works (DNNT) are documents that are still protected by copyright, but they are no longer available for purchase through commercial channels, e.g. bookstores. As such, they are included in the List of Out of Commerce Works kept by the National Library of the Czech Republic. Under the Out of Commerce Works license (DNNT), registered users may access, from a site in the Czech Republic, monographs published up to 2003 and periodicals published until 2013.

If you do not have login credentials, you need to register at one of the participating institutions. A list of such institutions, including information about remote registration, can be found here.

Out of Commerce Works (DNNT) are available to the registered users on the basis of licence agreements between National Library of the Czech Republic and the collective rights management organisations DILIA and OOA-S.

If you are unable to access a document under a license, then the general rules for non-public domain documents apply.

Logged-in users get remote access to documents that are protected by copyright but can be accessed under a license.

Logged-in users are allowed to access Out of Commerce Works (DNNT), i.e. monographs published in the Czech Republic before 2003 and newspapers and magazines published before 2013, if they are included in the List of Out of Commerce Works. These documents can be read, but not downloaded or printed.

Even without logging in it is possible to access:

  • documents licensed by the rights holder
  • documents that are no longer protected by copyright, which are those
    • published 110 years ago or more
    • for which it is verified that 70 years have passed since the death of all authors (including translator, illustrator, etc.) and 50 years since the publication of the document.

Such documents may be printed and downloaded.

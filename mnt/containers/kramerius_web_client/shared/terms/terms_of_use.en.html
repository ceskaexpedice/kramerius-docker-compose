Basic rights and responsibilities of the user

Documents protected by copyright law are in the Digital Library marked with a special bar with the text "Dílo nedostupné na trhu" or "Neveřejné dílo", basic user identification and a date.
  • The user is entitled to read documents contained in the Digital Library.
  • The user is obliged to behave in such a way that no violation or endangerment can occur when using the Digital Library concerning
    • personal rights
    • copyright of an author, heirs and executors of the rights to the document
  • When using the Digital Library the user may not:
    • interfere with the security, integrity and general operation of the digital library
    • run any kind of machine data mining processes
    • download, store, copy, distribute and provide to others (electronically or in printed form) documents to which he/she has gained access after login
    • use for business or other economic purposes the documents to which he/she has gained access after login
  • The user's account will be blocked if it is found:
    • non-standard use of the displayed documents
    • any kind of machine querying or processing of the digital library
  • If the user commits a breach of the terms of use and violation of the provisions of the Copyright Act the relevant institution with which the user is registered will provide the collective rights management organisations (DILIA, OOA-S) or the relevant right holders with all necessary data on the user and on the extent of the user's breach of obligations and on the extent of the damage or other harm suffered.

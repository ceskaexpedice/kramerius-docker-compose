How can I log in to the Digital Library?

You can log in to the Digital Library using eduID.

An overview of institutions whose registered users can log in remotely to the Digital Library can be found here. The last column indicates whether the institution allows remote registration. For further information, see the full FAQ on the National Library website.

Login via eduID:

  • check the acceptance of the terms and conditions and then click on the blue button "Login with library account/eduID"
  • choose your institution from the list
  • after selecting an institution, you will be redirected to its login page
  • log in with the credentials you use within your institution
  • if you click on the Login eduID button and only an empty list appears, just refresh the page with F5 or the appropriate browser icon
  • confirm your consent to the personal data sent
  • always log out of the application when you are finished using the link in the top right corner

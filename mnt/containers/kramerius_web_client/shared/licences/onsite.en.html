The document is not publicly accessible

The document you requested may not be freely viewed in accordance with the Czech Copyright Act (No. 121/2000 Col.) and is only accessible onsite in the premises of the library owning the printed copy, on dedicated terminals. The Copyright Act allows access to documents 70 years after the death of all authors (including the translator, illustrator, etc.) and 50 years after the publication of the document, beginning on 1st January of the year following the respective event. Monographs, newspapers and magazines for which the date of death of the authors is unknown shall be made available 110 years after publication. This procedure is based on the licence agreement between the National Library of the Czech Republic and the collective rights management organisations DILIA and OOA-S.

How do I access the document?

This document is accessible to registered users on computer stations in the library building.

I can't get to this library, how should I proceed?

The first option is to request a paper copy of the digital document from the National Library of the Czech Republic, if they have the same document in their own digital library. In this case, you can fill in the order form.. The second option is to go to your nearest library and, if they do not have a physical copy of the document, request it through interlibrary loan service. To find out about the availability of the document and the library that owns it, you can use the Czech central library portal Knihovny.cz.
